Any app. Zoom, Teams, Meet, FaceTime, WhatsApp, a browser tab. Microphone and system audio, both channels.

Speakers, live. You on one channel, the other side on the other. Words settle as soon as they are sure.

Offline. Parakeet runs on the Neural Engine of your Mac. No account, no upload, no cloud.

Written as you talk. Markdown, JSON and SRT land on disk segment by segment. A crash loses nothing.

Click the dot again or press ⌃⌥⌘R to stop.

Remote voices told apart after the call, and an echo gate keeps your mic from hearing the call twice.

Name it, or hand it straight to Claude, ChatGPT or any app with the prompt ready.
