# Distribution texts (go into the commit body of every draft)

Three short texts per piece, written in the same voice as the post, no hashtags, no emojis, no exclamation marks:
1. **LinkedIn**, three sentences: what the post answers, one concrete detail, the link. First person from Moritz.
2. **Reddit-style answer**: as a reply to someone asking the post's question in r/macapps, r/productivity or r/privacy. Answer the question directly in three to five sentences, mention Transcriber once as "I built", link at the end. Honest about limits.
3. **Newsletter paragraph**: 60 to 90 words, for a monthly email, plain.

These are drafts for Moritz to post by hand. The routine never posts anywhere.
